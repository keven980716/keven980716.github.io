I am a Ph.D. student at [Gaoling School of Artificial Intelligence](http://ai.ruc.edu.cn), [Renmin University of China](https://www.ruc.edu.cn), where I am advised by Prof. [Yankai Lin](https://linyankai.github.io). Before that, I got my Master's degree at the Center for Data Science of Peking University. I was a member of [LANCO](https://lancopku.github.io) group, where I was advised by Prof. [Xu Sun](https://xusun26.github.io/).

My research focuses on: (i) alignment and security problems of Large Language Models; (ii) Large Language Model reasoning. I'm always happy to discuss potential collaborations — feel free to reach out!
