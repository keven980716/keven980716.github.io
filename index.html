I am a PhD student at Gaoling School of Artificial Intelligence, Renmin University of China, where I am advised by Prof. Yankai Lin. Before that, I got my Master's degree at the Center for Data Science of Peking University. I was a member of LANCO group, where I was advised by Prof. Xu Sun.

My research focuses on: (i) alignment and security problems of Large Language Models; (ii) Large Language Model reasoning. I'm always happy to discuss potential collaborations—feel free to reach out!
